Guardian Shield — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Overview

Guardian Shield is a private family parental control app. It is not publicly available and is used exclusively within a single family unit. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Data We Collect

  • Account information — email address and password (for parent login via AWS Cognito)
  • Device information — device name, FCM token, and device identifiers (to send commands to the child device)
  • Usage data — app usage statistics and screen time data from the child device (to display in the parent dashboard)

How We Use Data

All data is used solely to provide parental control functionality within the family. Specifically:

  • To send lock/unlock commands to the child's device
  • To display screen time and app usage to the parent
  • To enforce bedtime schedules and app restrictions

Data Storage

Data is stored in AWS DynamoDB in the eu-west-1 (Ireland) region. All communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Authentication tokens are stored securely on-device using platform keystores (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties. The only external service used is Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for delivering commands to the child device.

Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained only while the family account is active. Deleting your account removes all associated data from our servers. To request account deletion, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Guardian Shield collects limited data from the child's device (usage statistics, device identifiers) solely for the purpose of parental oversight. No data is collected from children for marketing or advertising purposes.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to request data deletion, contact: privacy@battyesoft.com
